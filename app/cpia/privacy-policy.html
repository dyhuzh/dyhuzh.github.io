Cpia 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 8 月 25 日

中文 · English

概述

Cpia 是一款帮助个人用户记录、比较商品价格的 iOS 应用，由个人开发者独立开发。我们高度重视用户隐私，不收集、不存储、不出售任何个人信息。

数据存储方式

您在 Cpia 中创建的所有数据（品类、商品、价格、照片等）仅存储在您的设备本地，不会上传至任何服务器。

  • 数据保存于设备的沙盒存储（UserDefaults / 本地文件系统）中。
  • 备份与恢复通过 App 内的导出/导入功能，由用户自行管理，我们无法访问备份文件。

第三方服务：汇率 API

为提供货币换算功能，Cpia 会向 ExchangeRate-API（exchangerate-api.com）发送汇率请求。

  • 请求内容：仅包含基准货币代码（如 CNY），不包含任何用户标识、设备信息或个人数据。
  • 该服务由第三方提供，受其服务条款约束。
  • 用户可在设置页面选择填入自己的 API Key，该 Key 仅保存在本地设备，不经过我们的服务器。
网络请求是可选的——如不需要多币种比价，关闭汇率功能即可，App 不会自动联网。

应用内购买（IAP）

Cpia 提供一次性买断的完整版解锁。所有付款流程由 Apple App Store 处理，我们不接触任何支付信息或账单数据。

Apple 关于购买数据的隐私说明请参阅 Apple 隐私政策。

我们不收集的信息

  • 姓名、邮箱、电话等个人身份信息
  • 位置信息
  • 设备唯一标识符（IDFA / IDFV）
  • 崩溃报告或使用分析（App 内未集成任何分析 SDK）
  • 广告跟踪数据

儿童隐私

Cpia 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不有意收集任何儿童信息。

隐私政策变更

如本政策有重大变更，我们会在 App 更新说明中注明。继续使用即视为接受更新后的政策。

Cpia Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Overview

Cpia is an iOS app for personal price tracking and comparison, developed independently by a single developer. We take your privacy seriously and do not collect, store, or sell any personal information.

Data Storage

All data you create in Cpia (categories, products, prices, photos, etc.) is stored locally on your device only and is never uploaded to any server.

  • Data is saved in the app's sandboxed storage (UserDefaults / local file system).
  • Backup and restore are managed entirely by you using the export/import feature. We have no access to your backup files.

Third-Party Service: Exchange Rate API

To support currency conversion, Cpia sends requests to ExchangeRate-API (exchangerate-api.com).

  • Requests contain only a currency code (e.g. CNY). No user identifiers, device information, or personal data are included.
  • This service is operated by a third party and is subject to their Terms of Service.
  • You may optionally enter your own API key in Settings. It is stored locally on your device and never transmitted to our servers.
Network requests are optional. If you only use a single currency, no network calls are made.

In-App Purchases

Cpia offers a one-time purchase to unlock the full version. All payment processing is handled by Apple App Store. We do not have access to any payment or billing information.

For details on how Apple handles purchase data, see the Apple Privacy Policy.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers (IDFA / IDFV)
  • Crash reports or usage analytics (no analytics SDK is included)
  • Advertising or tracking data

Children's Privacy

Cpia is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

Changes to This Policy

If we make significant changes to this policy, we will note them in the App Store release notes. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
